The CUDA backend includes native sm_75 and sm_120a code plus compute_75 PTX for driver JIT on
intermediate NVIDIA architectures. IKUN reports devices below compute capability 7.5 as unsupported.

The standard installer discovers an installed NVIDIA CUDA 12.x toolkit and falls back to the
bundled CPU backend when it is unavailable. The separately named CUDA installer redistributes
cudart64_12.dll, cublas64_12.dll and cublasLt64_12.dll beside whisper.cpp under the NVIDIA CUDA
Toolkit EULA (see LICENSE.cuda.txt in that installer). nvcuda.dll remains part of the NVIDIA driver
and is never redistributed by IKUN.
